To check the status of all pods:
  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

To access Grafana:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-grafana 3000:80 -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  Then open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.

To access the ExaMon API:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-examon-server 5000:5000 -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

To access the MQTT broker:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-mosquitto 1883:1883 -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
