# Community

!!! warning "Status: Spec — section under construction"
    The Community landing is being authored. The about, credits, publications, contact, clusters, and releases pages already exist with their v0.5.0 content moved into them; this landing page is rewritten in a later slice.

> The Community section is the home of context that isn't about *using* or *running* ExaMon: who builds it, where it comes from, who has deployed it in production, what has been published about it, and how to get in touch. It also holds the release history and the cluster case studies.

## In this section

- **About** — what ExaMon is, where it comes from, the project mission.
- **Credits** — the people and institutions behind ExaMon.
- **Publications** — academic papers describing ExaMon and its use.
- **Contact** — how to reach the maintainers.
- **Clusters** — case studies of ExaMon deployments at Marconi 100 and Monte Cimone.
- **Releases** — release notes and historical release plans.
