> The Community section is the home of context that is not about using or running ExaMon: who builds it, where it comes from, who has deployed it in production, what has been published about it, and how to get in touch. It also holds the release history and the cluster case studies.

## In this section

- **About** — what ExaMon is, where it comes from, the project mission.
- **Credits** — the people and institutions behind ExaMon.
- **Publications** — academic papers describing ExaMon and its use.
- **Contact** — how to reach the maintainers.
- **Clusters** — case studies of ExaMon deployments at Marconi 100 and Monte Cimone.
- **Releases** — release notes and release plans.
